## Tenants Section

The Tenants section in your Tenant Workspace allows you to view and manage all tenants associated with your account. This guide will provide an overview of the information displayed.

### Overview of the Tenants Section

When you navigate to the Tenants section, you will see a list of all tenants associated with your account. Each tenant card displays the following information:

1. **Display Name:**

   - The friendly name assigned to the tenant for easy identification.

2. **System Name:**

   - The unique identifier used by the system for the tenant.

3. **Product:**

   - The product associated with the tenant. This indicates which product the tenant has subscribed to or is using.

4. **Creation Date:**

   - The date and time when the tenant was created.

5. **Last Update:**
   - The date and time of the most recent update to the tenant's information.

### Tenant Details

Each tenant card provides a quick summary of important details:

- **Display Name and System Name:**

  - These are shown prominently at the top of the tenant card to help you quickly identify the tenant.

- **Product:**

  - The product associated with the tenant is displayed with a product icon and name. This helps you understand which product the tenant is using.

- **Dates:**
  - Both the creation date and the last update date are shown. Hovering over the date provides more details in a tooltip format.

### Conclusion

The Tenants section in your Tenant Workspace provides a comprehensive overview of all your tenants and their associated products. It offers the necessary tools to manage tenant information effectively, ensuring that you can maintain a well-organized and efficient SaaS environment.
