## Tenant Workspace Introduction

Welcome to your Tenant Workspace on RoSaaS. This introduction will guide you through the features and navigation options available to you upon signing in.

### Signing In

1. **Sign In Page:**
   - Enter your registered email address and password.
   - Click the "Sign In" button to access your account.
   - If you don't have an account, you can create one by clicking the "Create Account" link.

### Workspace Overview

Upon signing in, you will land on the Workspace page. Here, you can manage all aspects of your subscription and account settings.

#### Main Sections of the Workspace

1. **Tenants**

   - **Description:** Manage and view details about the tenants associated with your account.
   - **Functionality:** Add new tenants, update tenant information, and manage tenant access levels.

2. **Subscriptions**

   - **Description:** Access and manage your active subscriptions.
   - **Functionality:** View subscription details, upgrade or downgrade plans, and manage subscription settings.

3. **Renewals**

   - **Description:** Review and manage subscription renewals.
   - **Functionality:** Enable or disable automatic renewals, review upcoming renewals, and manage renewal settings.

4. **Invoices**

   - **Description:** View and pay invoices associated with your subscriptions.
   - **Functionality:** Review invoice details, download invoices, and make payments directly from the workspace.

5. **Payment Methods**

   - **Description:** Manage your saved payment methods.
   - **Functionality:** Add new payment methods, update existing ones, and set default payment options.

6. **Profile**
   - **Description:** Manage your personal profile settings.
   - **Functionality:** Update personal information, change your password, and manage notification preferences.

### Navigation Bar

- **Profile Settings:**

  - Access your profile settings to update personal information, change your password, and manage notification preferences.

- **Go to Marketplace:**

  - Explore the Marketplace to discover new SaaS products and subscription plans.

- **Dark Mode:**

  - Toggle between light and dark mode for a comfortable viewing experience.

- **Language Options:**

  - Switch between different languages (e.g., Arabic) for a localized experience.

- **Logout:**
  - Sign out of your account securely.

### Workspace Widgets

- **Dashboard Widgets:**
  - The workspace includes various widgets that provide quick access to important information and actions. These widgets are designed to enhance your productivity and streamline your workflow.

### Footer

- **Footer Information:**
  - The footer includes copyright information and quick links to important sections of the RoSaaS platform.
