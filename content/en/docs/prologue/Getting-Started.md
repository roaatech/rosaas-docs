## Getting Started with RoSaaS

RoSaaS is designed to be intuitive and easy to use. This guide will help you understand the basic navigation and features available on the platform.

## Main Page

Upon accessing the RoSaaS platform, you will be greeted with the main page. Here you have two primary options:

- **Go to Marketplace**
- **Sign in**

### Go to Marketplace

Clicking on the "Go to Marketplace" button will take you to the Marketplace page, where you can explore and subscribe to various SaaS products. You can browse the available products even without signing in.

### Sign in

Clicking on the "Sign in" button will direct you to the login page where you can enter your credentials to access your account.

On the login page:

1. **Enter Your Email:** Input the email address you used to register with RoSaaS.
2. **Enter Your Password:** Input your secure password.
3. **Sign In:** Click the "Sign in" button to access your account.

If you don't have an account yet, you can create one by following the instructions in the Sign Up section.

### Sign Up

If you don't have an account, you can easily create one by clicking the "Create Account" link on the Sign in page. This will take you to the Sign-Up page where you can register as a tenant of a product.

On the Sign-Up page:

1. **Your Full Name:** Enter your full name.
2. **Your Phone:** Optionally, provide your phone number.
3. **Your Email:** Enter a valid email address which will be used for login and communication.
4. **Your Password:** Create a secure password for your account.
5. **Confirm Password:** Re-enter the password to confirm it.
6. **Sign Up:** Click the "Sign Up" button to complete the registration process.

After signing up, you will be able to log in and access your tenant dashboard where you can manage your subscriptions and other settings.
