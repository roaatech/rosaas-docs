# Product Subscriptions Tab

## Overview

The Product Subscriptions tab allows you to manage subscriptions for various product plans in RoSaaS. This tab provides detailed information about each subscription, including the plan, status, and important dates.

## Subscription Details

Each subscription entry includes the following details:

- **Title**: The name of the subscription.
- **Unique Name**: A unique identifier for the subscription.
- **Plan**: The specific plan associated with the subscription.
- **Subscription Status**: Indicates whether the subscription is active or inactive.
- **Start Date**: The date when the subscription starts.
- **End Date**: The date when the subscription ends.
- **Tenant Status**: The current status of the tenant associated with the subscription.
- **Created Date**: The date when the subscription was created.

## Managing Subscriptions

To manage a subscription, click on the "Manage" button in the Actions column. This will take you to the tenant page of the subscription where you can make further configurations and updates.

## Actions

For each subscription, the following actions are available:

- **View Details**: Provides detailed information about the subscription.
- **Edit**: Allows you to modify the subscription details.
- **Delete**: Removes the subscription from the system.

## Best Practices

- Regularly review subscription statuses to ensure that all active subscriptions are correctly configured.
- Use the "Manage" button to quickly access and update tenant details for each subscription.
- Keep track of subscription start and end dates to manage renewals and expirations effectively.

By efficiently managing product subscriptions, you can provide a seamless and organized experience for your tenants, ensuring that they have access to the appropriate plans and features.

## Summary

The Product Subscriptions tab is a crucial component for managing tenant access to your product plans. By understanding and utilizing the features of this tab, you can maintain an organized and efficient subscription management process in RoSaaS.
